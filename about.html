WHO WE ARE

Empowering people. Transforming communities.

Young Executive Minds (YEMs) is a registered Non-Profit Company based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

YEMs focuses on Social Enterprise Development Programmes aligned with Sustainable Development Goals 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 11.

Our main objectives are youth development, women empowerment and creating sustainable resilient communities through the Water, Sanitation and Health (WASH) protocol.

We double as a leadership accelerator and networking hub for youth and women from financially disadvantaged communities.

OUR MISSION

Revolutionise township economies

Through youth empowerment programmes.

OUR VISION

Future Executives

To transform the despondent to become Future Executives.

OUR GOAL

1500 Youth

Identify, train, coach, mentor and empower up to 1500 South African youth from townships by 2026.
