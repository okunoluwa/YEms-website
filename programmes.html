OUR WORK

Programmes That Create Opportunities

Developing skills, leadership and sustainable township economies.

01 / ENTREPRENEURSHIP

YEMs Business Accelerator Network (YBAN)

A township entrepreneurship advocacy programme designed to train individuals to start SMMEs, access information, opportunities, markets and finance.

The programme also brings youth leaders together with experts and industry leaders for networking, business advice and mentorship.

02 / GLOBAL EXPOSURE

International Youth Exchange Programme

This programme exposes selected youth leaders to global platforms where they can learn and adopt best practices in business processes from different regions.

YEMs partners with individuals, institutions and organisations within and beyond South Africa to create learning and networking opportunities.

03 / COMMUNITY HEALTH

Community Water, Sanitation & Health (WASH)

This programme supports water, sanitation and health initiatives in communities, including water treatment, recycling, sanitation facilities, local supplier engagement, skills development and job creation.

It also promotes WASH education in local school communities.

04 / SKILLS & PRODUCTION

Local Community Manufacturing Programme

Developing practical manufacturing skills through activities such as perfume making, soap making, body lotion production, cleaning agents and furniture making.

05 / RESEARCH

Young Researchers Programme (YRP)

A youth capacity development programme that teaches young people to develop ideas, ask critical questions, investigate community issues and turn their findings into written publications or development projects.

06 / DIGITAL SKILLS

Technology & Innovation Programme

Building technology capabilities in web design, computer programming, application development, Microsoft Office and database administration.

Beneficiaries are also expected to train other young women in technology skills and develop personalised applications that can be patented and commercialised.

07 / CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Youth Internship Training Programme

Helping young people identify their interests, strengths and weaknesses while developing essential soft and hard skills for their chosen industries.

The programme includes personality profiling, training and workplace internship opportunities.

08 / BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Sales & Distributorship Programme

Identifying and training individuals interested in sales, developing sales teams, teaching physical and digital sales methods, and supporting product distribution and sales performance monitoring.
