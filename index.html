WHO WE ARE

Building a generation of leaders and innovators.

Young Executive Minds (YEMs) is a registered Non-Profit Company based in Johannesburg, South Africa. The organisation focuses on social enterprise development programmes aligned with Sustainable Development Goals 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 11.
